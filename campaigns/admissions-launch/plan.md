# Campaign Plan: Admissions 2026 Now Open

**College:** IMCG, Humak
**Channel:** email only
**Campaign window:** Mon 7 Dec 2026 to Sun 3 Jan 2027 (4 weeks)
**Prepared:** 9 Nov 2026

> Anything in **[square brackets]** must be confirmed by the college before an email goes out. Our brand rules forbid unconfirmed dates, fees or seat numbers.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Admissions 2026 Now Open
- **Summary:** A four-week email campaign that tells everyone on our admissions waitlist that applications are open, explains each programme, walks them through applying, and reminds them calmly of the deadline.
- **Primary goal:** **2,000 applications submitted by waitlist members by Sun 3 Jan 2027.**
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Every waitlist member opens at least one campaign email.
  - Fewer incomplete applications, because families know which documents to prepare.
  - Fewer repeated phone questions to 051-4493301, because the emails answer them first.

> **Note on the goal:** the waitlist is about 2,000 people, so this goal means *every* member applies. A typical email campaign converts far fewer. Keep 2,000 as the stretch goal, and see "Risks" for a working target.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 aspirants who joined our admissions waitlist, in three groups:

| Group | Who they are | What they care about most |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FSc** (Pre-Medical / Pre-Engineering) | Students aiming for medicine or engineering, and their parents | Science labs, professional faculty, a serious learning culture, preparation for entry tests |
| **FA** (Arts and Humanities) | Students drawn to languages, social sciences and the arts, and their parents | Subject choices, a caring environment, room to grow in writing, debate and the arts |
| **ICS** (Computer Science) | Students interested in computing and maths, and their parents | Computer labs, practical work, a clear path to further study in computing |

**Shared concerns across all groups:**
- Is the college safe, disciplined and well run? (Parents of a daughter care about this most.)
- Exactly how do I apply, and what do I need?
- When is the deadline, and what happens after I apply?

**Stage:** decision. They already joined the waitlist, so they're interested. The campaign removes doubt and makes applying easy.

**Who reads the email:** often a parent, sometimes the student. Emails speak to the family: "you" for the reader, "your daughter" where we address parents.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Admissions for 2026 are open at IMCG, Humak. Here is how your daughter can apply.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **A learning culture you can trust.** Professional faculty and our best efforts for every student.
   *Proof:* faculty qualifications, labs and facilities on the college website [confirm the details to mention].
2. **A programme for every path.** FSc, FA and ICS, each with its own subjects and future options.
   *Proof:* the subject list for each programme [from the admissions office].
3. **Applying is simple.** Clear steps, a documents checklist, and help by phone.
   *Proof:* a step-by-step guide and the phone line 051-4493301.
4. **We follow the rules and protect privacy.** Admissions follow the guidelines of the relevant authorities, and applicant details stay private.
   *Proof:* a statement of the admissions policy [confirm the wording with the college].

**Tone by group:** the message stays the same. Only the examples change: labs and entry tests for FSc, subjects and expression for FA, computing for ICS. The tone is warm and formal for parents. An occasional Urdu greeting ("Assalam-o-Alaikum") is fine.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** (only channel) | The waitlist gave us their email. It's free, direct and measurable. | 4 plain, mobile-friendly emails, one main link each, one version per group | Low to medium | Staff time only |

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How we track it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Applications submitted (primary)** | **2,000** (stretch); **600** working target, see Risks | Application form records, with the waitlist group noted |

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **The goal is 100% of the waitlist.** Some members will choose another college, already have a place elsewhere, or have joined with an old address. | Report the 2,000 stretch goal alongside a **working target of 600 (30%)**. Review after week 2 and add the optional extra reminder if applications are low. |
| **Winter holidays and Quaid-e-Azam Day** fall in weeks 3 and 4, when families travel and read less email. | Send on Mondays, not near 25 Dec. Keep emails short. Make sure the application form works on a phone. |
| **Unconfirmed details** (deadline, fees, seats, subject lists) break our "no unconfirmed promises" rule. | Every email uses [brackets] until the college confirms. No email goes out with a bracket still in it. |
| **Name confusion:** "Admissions 2026" goes out in Dec 2026 to Jan 2027, so families may wonder which session it's for. | Confirm the session name, for example "2026–28 session" or "2027 intake", and use it the same way in every email. |
