# IMCG, Humak: Brand Voice

How Islamabad Model College for Girls, Humak sounds in every email. After reading an email from us, parents, officials and faculty should feel **informed, included and reassured**, and see the college as credible, professional and responsible.

Call the college **IMCG, Humak** in emails.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Credible. Caring. Professional.**

How the tone changes for each audience, while the voice stays the same:

| Audience | Tone | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parents | Warm and formal | Respectful and reassuring. Speak about "your daughter". Use an Urdu greeting or line now and then, for example "Assalam-o-Alaikum". |
| Officials | Strictly formal | Brief, factual, correct titles. No warmth words, no Urdu, no exclamation marks. |
| Faculty | Collegial | Direct and appreciative, as one colleague to another. |

Write in English most of the time. Urdu is only for parents, and only occasionally.

## 2. Words we use

- "our students"
- "your daughter" (to parents)
- "our rising stars" (about students' achievements)
- "IMCG, Humak" (for the college)
- "best efforts", "professional faculty", "learning culture"
- "in line with the guidelines of" the relevant authority
- "Assalam-o-Alaikum" (occasionally, for parents)

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Slang and casual words:** "hey", "guys", "awesome", "cool".
- **Hype:** "amazing", "best in Islamabad", "world-class", "number one", lots of exclamation marks.
- **Pushy sales words:** "hurry", "don't miss out", "act now", "last chance". The one exception is a real admissions deadline or seat limit, stated calmly and factually, for example "Admissions close on 15 March."
- **Corporate words:** "customers", "clients", "leads", "offer", "deal", "package", "leverage", "synergy". We serve students and families. We don't sell to customers.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No guaranteed results:** no "100% pass rate", "guaranteed admission to medical college" or "every student succeeds".
- **No rankings or comparisons:** no "best college in Islamabad", and no "better than" another school or college.
- **No unconfirmed promises** about admissions, scholarships, fees or dates.
- **Never share student details:** no names, photos or results without permission.
- **Never speak for official bodies:** the Federal Directorate of Education, FBISE or any other authority. We say we follow their guidelines; we don't speak for them.

**What we can say instead:** the college makes its best efforts, with professional faculty and a strong learning culture. It protects student privacy. It follows the guidelines of the relevant authorities.
